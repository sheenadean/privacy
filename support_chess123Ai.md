### Before you email

**My opponent disappeared, or the game froze mid-match**
Online games automatically resync when the app comes back to the foreground. If your opponent's
connection actually drops, the game auto-forfeits in their favor after about 2 minutes of no
activity — no need to report it.

**I want to take back a move**
Undo only works against the built-in AI. It's disabled for online games so both players are
always looking at the same, agreed-upon position.

**I want my account and data gone**
In the app, go to `Settings → Delete Account`. This removes your profile, stats, friends, and
pending invites immediately — no need to email first.

**Is my match history stored anywhere?**
No — we don't keep a record of finished games on our servers. You can download a copy to your
own device right after any game ends.

**Someone is harassing me, or I suspect cheating**
Block or report them directly from their row in Contacts (swipe left). Reports go to us for
review and aren't visible to the person reported.
