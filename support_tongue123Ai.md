## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does the analysis work?
Take or upload a photo of your tongue. The app sends it to our backend, which uses AI (Anthropic's Claude) to generate a Traditional Chinese Medicine–style reference reading — tongue color, coating, teeth marks, organ-zone notes, and pattern insight. Results are generated fresh each time and are not stored on our servers.

**This is a wellness/educational reference tool only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or provide medical advice.** Always consult a licensed physician for health concerns.

### My photo wasn't recognized as a tongue
Make sure your tongue is extended, well-lit, and fills most of the frame (see the "Example" photo in the app for reference). Avoid heavy shadows or motion blur. If it keeps failing on a clear photo, email us with your Device ID.

### I ran out of scans for today
Free and Lite plans include 3 scans/day; Monthly/Yearly include 15/day; Pro plans include 50/day. Your count resets at your device's local midnight. Upgrading your plan in **Pricing** unlocks a higher daily limit immediately.

### How do I subscribe, change, or cancel my plan?
Subscriptions are managed entirely through your Apple ID, not inside the app:
1. Open the **Settings** app on your iPhone
2. Tap your name → **Subscriptions**
3. Select **Tongue123Ai** to upgrade, downgrade, or cancel

Canceling stops future renewals — you keep your current plan's access until the end of the paid period.

### I reinstalled the app / got a new phone and lost my subscription
Open **Pricing → Restore Purchases**. As long as you're signed into the same Apple ID that made the purchase, your plan is restored automatically — no account or login needed.

### Can I get a refund?
Refunds for App Store purchases are handled entirely by Apple, not by us. Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) or via **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions** on your device.

### Is my data private?
Yes — there's no account, no login, and no name/email collected. Tongue photos are processed only to generate your result and are not permanently stored. See our full [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) for details.

### The app isn't loading results / seems stuck
Check your internet connection and try again. If the issue persists, email us with your Device ID and roughly when it happened, and we'll look into it.
