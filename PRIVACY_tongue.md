Privacy Policy
Effective date: August 2, 2026

Tongue Analysis ("the App") is a wellness reference tool that uses AI to analyze photos of the tongue according to Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) principles. This policy explains what data the App collects, why, and how it's used.

The App is for wellness reference and educational purposes only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical concerns.

1. Information We Collect
Device identifier — an anonymous ID generated by your device (identifierForVendor), not tied to your name, email, or Apple ID. Used to enforce daily scan limits and to know which subscription plan applies to your device.
Tongue photos — when you submit a photo for analysis, it is sent to our backend and then to a third-party AI provider (Anthropic, maker of Claude) to generate your results. Photos are processed transiently for the sole purpose of generating your analysis and are not permanently stored on our servers.
Subscription status — if you subscribe, we record your plan and its expiration date (synced from Apple's StoreKit) against your device identifier, so we can enforce the correct usage limits. This includes StoreKit's transaction identifiers, which let a subscription be recognized again after a reinstall or on a new device signed into the same Apple ID — without any login or account system of our own.
App language — your device's language setting (or a language you choose in-app) is sent with each analysis request so results are generated in that language, and may be used to fetch a cached UI translation. No personal data is included in this.
We do not require or collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifying account information. There is no user account, sign-in, or password.
2. How We Use Information
To generate your tongue analysis results.
To enforce free-trial and subscription usage limits.
To recognize an existing subscription across reinstalls or devices (via Apple's StoreKit, not a login).
To improve the accuracy and speed of the AI analysis over time.
3. Third Parties
Anthropic (Claude API) processes your photo (and, separately, non-personal UI text for translation) to generate results. See Anthropic's privacy policy for how they handle API data.
Apple (StoreKit) processes subscription purchases; we never see or store your payment details.
4. Data Retention
Photos are not stored after analysis completes.
Device identifier, subscription plan, and daily scan counts are retained on our servers for as long as needed to operate the App's usage limits.
Cached UI translations and language preference are stored locally on your device only.
5. Your Choices
You can reset your local App data at any time from Settings → Reset app state.
Because there is no account system, deleting the App removes your local data; a new install generates a new device identifier (though an active subscription will be recognized again automatically via StoreKit, as described above).
6. If We Discontinue the Service
We do not guarantee the App or its backend will be available indefinitely. If we discontinue the App or its supporting servers: we stop processing photos entirely (we never retained them anyway); any device-linked usage data held on our servers is deleted or rendered inaccessible; you should cancel any active auto-renewing subscription yourself via your Apple ID account settings, since we cannot cancel it on your behalf — refunds for any period after discontinuation, if applicable, are handled by Apple per its own policies; previously-downloaded copies of the App may still open and show past results, but new analyses will no longer be possible once the backend is offline.

7. Children's Privacy
The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Security
Data in transit to our backend and to Anthropic is encrypted (HTTPS). We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising purposes.

9. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date above.
