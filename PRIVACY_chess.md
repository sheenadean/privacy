# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 6, 2026

This privacy policy explains what information the Chess app ("the app") collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

## Who runs this app

This app is developed and operated by an independent developer. If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact:

**deanteam.ai@gmail.com**

## What we collect

The app uses [Supabase](https://supabase.com) (a third-party backend service) to power its online features. When you use those features, the following is stored:

| Data | Why we collect it |
|---|---|
| An anonymous account identifier (a random ID, not tied to your name, email, or Apple ID) | To let you sign back in and keep your data across sessions, without asking for any personal information |
| A display name you choose | Shown to other players in Contacts, invites, and match history |
| Game statistics (ELO rating, wins, losses, draws) | To track your rank and match you with similarly-skilled opponents |
| Friend relationships (who you've added, blocked) | To power the Contacts / friends list feature |
| Match history (moves, results, timestamps) | So you and your opponents can see past games |
| Online/presence status (a timestamp of when the app was last active) | To show friends whether you're currently online |
| Reports you file against other users | To review and act on abuse reports (see "Reporting & Safety" below) |

We do **not** collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, or any device identifiers used for advertising. We do not use the app to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Account creation

The app automatically creates an anonymous account the first time you open it — there's no sign-up form, no password, and no personal information requested. If a future version adds Sign in with Apple or another login method, this policy will be updated first.

## Reporting & Safety

If you report another user, the report (who filed it, who it's about, and the reason) is stored so it can be reviewed. Reports are not visible to other users, including the person being reported. You can also block other users, which hides them from your friends list and stops them from interacting with you through the app.

Accounts found to violate the app's Terms of Use may be suspended or removed at the developer's discretion.

## How to delete your data

You can delete your account at any time from **Settings → Delete Account**. This immediately:

- Removes your display name and resets your stats
- Removes all your friend relationships and pending invites
- Removes your matchmaking queue entry and online-status record
- Signs you out

Completed match history is kept so your past opponents' records stay intact, but your name in that history is replaced with "Deleted User" — nothing personally identifying remains attached to it.

## Third-party services

The app's backend runs on Supabase, which stores the data described above on its servers. See [Supabase's own privacy policy](https://supabase.com/privacy) for how they handle data on their platform. We don't share your data with any other third party, and we don't run ads or use analytics/tracking SDKs.

## Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top will change, and material changes will be reflected in the app's release notes.
