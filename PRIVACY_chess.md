# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Chess123Ai uses Supabase, a third-party backend service, to power its online features. When you
use those features, the following is stored:

**What we store**
- An anonymous account identifier — a random ID, not tied to your name, email, or Apple ID
- A display name you choose, shown to other players in Contacts and invites
- Game statistics: ELO rating, wins, losses, draws
- Friend relationships — who you've added, blocked
- Online/presence status — a timestamp of when the app was last active, shown to friends
- Reports you file against other users, so they can be reviewed
- AI hint credit balance and Online subscription status, if you purchase either

**What we don't collect**
Your name, email, phone number, precise location, or advertising identifiers. We don't track you
across other apps or websites.

## Match/game history is not retained

We do not keep a record of your finished games — no move-by-move history, no list of past
opponents or results is stored on our servers after a game ends. Right after a game (online or
against the AI), you can download a plain-text record of it to your own device — that's the only
copy that exists, and it's entirely up to you whether to keep it.

## Account creation

The app automatically creates an anonymous account the first time you open it — no sign-up form,
no password, no personal information requested.

## Reporting & safety

Reports you file (who it's about, and why) are stored for review and are not visible to other
users, including the person reported. You can block other users at any time from Contacts.

## Deleting your data

Settings → Delete Account immediately clears your display name, stats, friend relationships,
pending invites, queue entry, and online-status record, then signs you out.

## Third-party services

Data described above is stored on Supabase's servers — see [supabase.com/privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy) for their own
policy. We don't share your data with anyone else, and we don't run ads or analytics/tracking
SDKs.

## Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from children
under 13.
