Stock123Ai — Privacy Policy
Last updated: 2026-08-05

What we collect
Device identifier — a per-app, per-device ID (iOS's identifierForVendor, not your Apple ID or phone number) used to enforce free-trial and daily usage limits. Reset if you uninstall and reinstall the app.
Chart images you upload — sent to our server to be analyzed, and forwarded to Anthropic's Claude API to generate the analysis. We do not permanently store these images; they're processed in memory for the duration of the request and discarded.
Usage counts and timestamps — how many analyses you've run today/this month, tied to your device identifier, so we can enforce plan limits.
Subscription status — handled entirely by Apple's App Store / StoreKit. We never see your payment details; Apple tells our server only whether a subscription is active.
What we don't collect
The iOS app has no account/login system — we don't collect your name, email, or Apple ID unless you separately contact us by email. We don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell any data to third parties.

Third parties
Anthropic (creator of the Claude models) processes the chart images and text you submit for analysis, per Anthropic's own privacy policy. Apple processes all payment/subscription data per Apple's own policies — we never handle your card details.

Data retention
Device-identifier-linked usage counts are kept indefinitely to enforce plan limits, unless you request deletion (see Contact below).

Children's privacy
This app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Stock123Ai — 隐私政策
最后更新：2026-08-05

我们收集什么
设备标识符 —— App 专用的每设备 ID（iOS 的 identifierForVendor，不是你的 Apple ID 或手机号），用来控制免费试用和每日用量上限。卸载重装会重置。
你上传的图表截图 —— 发送到我们的服务器做分析，并转发给 Anthropic 的 Claude API 生成分析结果。我们不会永久存储这些图片，只在处理这次请求期间保留在内存里，处理完即丢弃。
用量次数和时间戳 —— 你今天/本月分析了几次，跟设备标识符关联，用来执行套餐限额。
订阅状态 —— 完全由苹果 App Store / StoreKit 处理，我们看不到你的付款信息，苹果只告诉我们服务器"这个订阅现在是否有效"。
我们不收集什么
iOS App 没有账号/登录系统——除非你主动通过邮件联系我们，否则我们不会收集你的姓名、邮箱或 Apple ID。我们不会跨其它 App 或网站追踪你，也不会把任何数据卖给第三方。

第三方
Anthropic（Claude 模型的开发方）会按照 Anthropic 自己的隐私政策 处理你提交用来分析的图表和文字。苹果按苹果自己的政策处理全部付款/订阅数据——我们不会接触到你的银行卡信息。

数据保留
跟设备标识符关联的用量计数会一直保留，用来执行套餐限额，除非你主动要求删除（见下方"联系我们"）。

儿童隐私
本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会主动收集他们的数据。
