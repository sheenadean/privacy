# Stock123Ai — Support

Need help with Stock123Ai? Start here.

## What Stock123Ai does

Stock123Ai reads a screenshot of a candlestick chart and gives you two independent analyses side by side: an AI vision read (trend, support/resistance, candlestick patterns, a buy/sell/hold call, and suggested entry/stop-loss/target when there's a clear setup) and a computer-vision signal engine that reconstructs the price data and runs classic technical indicators (moving averages, MACD, KD, Bollinger Bands) independently.

## How to use it

1. Open the **Analyze** tab and tap **Photo Library** (or **Camera**) to pick a chart screenshot. Make sure the current price is visible in the image — the app needs it to calibrate the price scale.
2. Crop to just the chart if you want, or analyze the whole screenshot as-is.
3. The result streams in: your photo shows immediately, then the full analysis (verdict, levels, reasoning) fills in over the next several seconds.

## Free trial & subscriptions

- The **3-day free trial** (3 analyses/day) starts when you tap "Choose" on the free plan card in the **Pricing** tab — it does not start automatically on install.
- Paid plans are billed through your Apple ID via the App Store, and auto-renew until canceled. Manage or cancel anytime in **Settings app → your name → Subscriptions** on your iPhone (not inside Stock123Ai — Apple handles all billing).
- **Restore Purchases**: if you reinstall the app or switch to a new iPhone signed into the same Apple ID, tap "Restore Purchases" at the top of the Pricing tab to reconnect your existing subscription.
- The free trial itself is tied to your device, not your Apple ID — it does not carry over to a new device or a reinstall after uninstalling every app from this developer.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Couldn't read that image"** — the app couldn't recognize the picture as a price chart. Try a clearer screenshot, ideally one that shows the current price and a reasonable amount of price history.
- **"Today's/this month's quota is used up"** — you've hit your plan's daily or monthly analysis limit. It resets at your device's local midnight; upgrading to a higher tier in Pricing raises the limit immediately.
- **A request fails or times out occasionally** — this usually clears up on retry. Our analysis server can take a little longer to respond right after being idle; if it keeps happening, please email us with the approximate time it occurred.

## Stock123Ai 是做什么的

Stock123Ai 读取一张K线图截图，同屏给你两种独立的分析：AI 视觉分析（趋势、支撑/阻力位、K线形态、买/卖/持有判断，行情明确时还会给出进场价/止损价/目标价）和计算机视觉信号引擎（从截图反推出真实价格数据，独立跑一遍均线、MACD、KD、布林带等经典技术指标）。

## 使用方式

1. 打开**分析**标签页，点**相册**（或**拍照**）选一张图表截图。确保截图里能看到当前价格——App 需要它来校准价格刻度。
2. 需要的话裁一下图，也可以直接用整张截图分析。
3. 结果是流式出来的：你选完照片那一刻图就显示出来，完整分析（判断、关键价位、理由）会在接下来几秒钟内跟上。

## 常见问题排查

- **"图片无法识别，换一张试试"** —— App 没能认出这是一张价格走势图。换一张更清晰的截图，最好能看到当前价格和一段完整的价格走势。
- **"今日/本月次数已用完"** —— 已经达到你所在套餐的每日/每月分析次数上限，会在你设备的本地时间午夜重置；在订阅页升级到更高档位会立即提高额度。
- **偶尔请求失败或超时** —— 一般重试一次就好。分析服务器闲置一段时间后重新被唤醒会稍慢一点；如果反复发生，请把大概发生的时间发邮件告诉我们。
